This week I had a great time attending the Conference on [Computational Methods in Water Resources](https://cmwrconference.org/2026/) in Bologna, Italy, where I presented my work in progress on agroforestry light modelling.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/presenting.jpeg" alt="Presenting at the CMWR 2026 conference" caption="Presenting at the CMWR 2026 conference" %}

The conference was really well put together, and it was probably the first one where I felt like I could engage a bit better with the other sessions too. Everyone was working on computation and water resources in some form, so even talks far from my own corner of the field felt accessible. I also had a wonderful time exploring Bologna, wandering under the porticoes and eating far too well between sessions. Looking forward to seeing where the conference takes us next time!
